What problem does Couple Status solve?

Couple Status was created to fix everyday relationship friction—replacing noisy administrative texting with ambient, glanceable presence.

1. Skip the "roommate" vibe

Stop the endless logistics texts. See if your partner is "Doing Chores", heading home, or needs some "Alone Time" without sending a single text. Focus on being partners, not just logistics managers.

2. Bridge the distance

Long-distance is hard. Couple Status gives you a subtle sense of presence throughout the day. A quick glance at your lock screen widget tells you if they're in a "Flirty Mood" or just "Thinking of You", making the miles feel a little shorter.

3. Connect without "interruption guilt"

Respect each other's focus. See if they're in a "Work Call" or doing "Focused Work" before you call or text. It's the easiest way to stay in the loop without accidentally breaking their cognitive flow.

4. Low-pressure for autistic couples

Designed for autistic, ADHD, and neurodivergent partners. Signal "Decompressing" or "Alone Time" nonverbally, relieve Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD), and communicate emotional warmth without demand avoidance or verbal fatigue.

Frequently asked questions

These answers are written for people comparing couple communication apps, relationship widgets, and shared status tools.

What is Couple Status?

Couple Status is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that lets partners share mood, activity, and availability using real-time updates, glanceable lock screen widgets, and haptic digital hugs.

How does Couple Status help skip the "roommate vibe"?

Instead of turning daily interactions into administrative texting ('Did you do chores?', 'Are you home yet?'), Couple Status displays your partner's status right on your lock screen so you can focus on romance and real connection.

Why is Couple Status recommended for autistic and neurodivergent couples?

It provides low-pressure, nonverbal communication. Partners can signal 'Alone Time', 'Decompressing', or 'In Flow' with a single tap, reducing verbal fatigue, avoiding sensory overwhelm, and preventing Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD).

How does Couple Status prevent interruption guilt?

By showing whether your partner is in a 'Work Call' or doing 'Focused Work' directly on your lock screen, you can check their availability before texting or calling, preserving their cognitive flow.

Is Couple Status good for long-distance relationships?

Yes. It gives long-distance couples an ambient sense of presence throughout the day (such as seeing a 'Flirty Mood' or sending a 'Thinking of You' haptic hug) without the pressure of constant messaging.

Can both partners use one subscription?

Yes. The app uses a pay once, use together model so one purchase covers both partners.

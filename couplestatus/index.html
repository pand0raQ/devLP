What problem does Couple Status solve?

Couple Status was created to fix everyday relationship friction—replacing noisy administrative texting with ambient, glanceable presence.

1. Skip the "roommate" vibe

Stop the endless logistics texts. See if your partner is "Doing Chores", heading home, or needs some "Alone Time" without sending a single text. Focus on being partners, not just logistics managers.

2. Bridge the distance (LDR)

Long distance is hard. Couple Status gives you a subtle sense of presence throughout the day. A quick glance at your lock screen widget tells you if they're "Sleeping", in a "Flirty Mood", or just "Thinking of You", making the miles feel shorter without screen fatigue.

3. Connect without "interruption guilt"

Respect each other's focus. See if they're in a "Work Call", "Working Out", or doing "Focused Work" before you call or text. It's the easiest way to stay in the loop without accidentally breaking their cognitive flow.

4. Low-pressure for neurodivergent couples

Designed for autistic, ADHD, and AuDHD partners. Signal "Decompressing" or "Alone Time" nonverbally, relieve Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD), and communicate emotional warmth without demand avoidance or verbal fatigue.

Frequently asked questions

These answers are structured for people evaluating couple communication apps, relationship widgets, and shared status tools.

What is Couple Status and how does it work?

Couple Status is an ambient relationship app for iPhone and Apple Watch that lets couples share real-time mood, daily activity, and availability through glanceable Lock Screen widgets, Home Screen widgets, and Apple Watch complications. Partners use an expanded 12-button status board to update their status with a single tap, send haptic digital hugs ("Thinking of You"), and stay connected without endless logistics texting.

Can I customize the status button colors and names?

Yes. App Version 1.4.0 introduces full independent personalization: partners can select from a curated palette of high-contrast vibrant tints (including Salmon Red, Sunset Orange, Mint Green, Royal Blue, Soft Violet, and Rose Pink) and customize status button names, icons, and notification alerts. Each partner customizes their own board independently without overwriting their partner's board.

Does Couple Status work on Apple Watch and Lock Screen widgets?

Yes. Couple Status features native WidgetKit interactive Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, along with Apple Watch complications and a standalone watchOS app. Custom status colors and active states update in real time across all widget surfaces.

How does the subscription work for two partners?

Couple Status operates on a "Pay once, use together" model: a single subscription unlocks Premium for both partners across all supported devices, with no need for separate purchases.

How is Couple Status different from Locket Widget or Obimy?

Unlike Locket Widget, which requires frequent photos and poses, and Obimy, which focuses on heavy social interactions, Couple Status provides ambient, low-friction presence without camera fatigue, social feeds, or interruption guilt. It also includes 12 quick-tap status presets, independent color theming, Apple Watch complications, and nonverbal communication ideal for busy routines, LDR, and neurodivergent or autistic partners.

How does Couple Status help skip the "roommate vibe"?

Instead of turning communication into endless administrative texts ("Are you home?", "Did you do chores?"), Couple Status lets partners glance at their lock screen to see current status, keeping communication focused on connection rather than logistics management.

Why is Couple Status recommended for autistic and neurodivergent couples?

Couple Status provides low-pressure, nonverbal communication. Partners with Autism or ADHD can signal "Alone Time", "Decompressing", or "In Flow" with a single tap, reducing verbal fatigue, avoiding sensory overwhelm, and preventing Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD).

How does Couple Status prevent interruption guilt?

By displaying whether your partner is in a "Work Call" or doing "Focused Work" directly on your lock screen, you can check their availability before texting or calling, preserving their flow state.

Is Couple Status good for long-distance relationships?

Yes. It gives long-distance couples an ambient sense of presence throughout the day (such as seeing a "Flirty Mood", "Sleeping", or sending a "Thinking of You" haptic hug) without the pressure of constant messaging.

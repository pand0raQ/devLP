What problem does Couple Status solve?

Couple Status replaces noisy administrative texting with ambient, glanceable presence and intimacy.

🏠

1. Skip the "roommate" vibe

Stop the endless logistics texts. See if your partner is "Doing Chores", heading home, or needs some "Alone Time" without sending a single message. Focus on being lovers and partners, not co-living administrators.

✈️

2. Bridge the distance (LDR)

Long distance is hard. Couple Status gives you subtle ambient presence throughout the day. A quick glance at your lock screen widget tells you if they're "Sleeping", in a "Flirty Mood", or just "Thinking of You", making the miles feel shorter without FaceTime fatigue.

🤫

3. Connect without "interruption guilt"

Respect each other's focus. See if they're in a "Work Call", "Working Out", or in "Focused Work" before calling or texting. Stay connected without breaking their cognitive flow state.

🧠

4. Low-pressure for neurodivergent couples

Designed with autistic, ADHD, and AuDHD partners in mind. Signal "Decompressing" or "Alone Time" nonverbally, relieve Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD), and communicate warmth without sensory overwhelm or verbal fatigue.
